# Email (Resend)

How Tracelings email works, for future sessions.

- **Provider:** Resend. Sends from `hello@tracelings.com` (tracelings.com is verified in Resend). Plan: free marketing tier (1,000 contacts, 3 segments; Resend's own "General" segment uses one).
- **Segments:** `prospects` (families, not members, get launch and sales emails) and `teachers` (honest classroom note only). Members are marked with the `member` contact property; the welcome series stops at the next step for members and for anyone who unsubscribes.
- **Sign-ups:** `/api/subscribe/` sends to Zapier (alerts) and Resend (contact + welcome trigger). Never blocks a download.
- **Welcome series:** Resend Automations, one per interest (letters, coloring, math, homeschool, program, general, teacher), days 0, 2, 5, 9, 14. Created switched off; turned on with the admin action `automations-enable` after Jesse approves.
- **Templates:** `src/lib/email/templates.ts`. `setup` pushes and publishes them to Resend. Preview with `npx tsx scripts/email-preview.mts <dir>`.
- **Launch broadcasts** for the imported Oct 2026 list: drafts `launch-a`, `launch-a-teacher`, `launch-b`, `launch-c`. Sent only with `broadcast-send` and `confirm: "SEND"`.
- **Re-creating a launch draft** after the copy changes: `broadcast-create` with the same template refreshes the existing draft of that name in place (no duplicates; pass `fresh: true` to force a new one). A scheduled one is refused: run `broadcast-cancel` with `{ id }` first (Resend `POST /broadcasts/{id}/cancel`: scheduled goes back to draft, queued becomes canceled), then `broadcast-create` again, then `broadcast-send` to reschedule.
- **Copy rule since Oct 24, 2026:** emails never promise the homeschool plans stay free. Use: "Every one-page printable on Tracelings stays free. The full homeschool plans now live inside the membership, and week 1 is always free to try." Devices that used the free plans keep them free through Nov 30, 2026 (launch-a says so).
- **Admin API:** `POST https://tracelings.com/api/admin/email/` with `Authorization: Bearer $EMAIL_ADMIN_TOKEN`. Actions: domain-status, domain-verify, setup, automations-enable/disable, import, broadcast-create, broadcast-cancel, broadcast-send, test-send (only to OWNER_EMAIL), templates, stats, inbound-webhook, welcome-backfill, digest.
- **Replies to hello@tracelings.com:** Resend receiving (enabled on the domain in the Resend dashboard) calls `/api/email/inbound/` (signed with RESEND_WEBHOOK_SECRET), which forwards to OWNER_EMAIL with Reply-To set to the sender.
- **Daily download digest:** one email a day to OWNER_EMAIL from `Tracelings reports <hello@tracelings.com>` with yesterday's (America/Chicago) downloads grouped by visitor, top pages, new sign-ups and checkout starts. Vercel Cron (`vercel.json`, `5 12 * * *` UTC, about 7am Central) calls `GET /api/cron/daily-digest/` with `Authorization: Bearer $CRON_SECRET`; nothing is sent on an empty day unless `?force=1`. Records are small private blobs (`events/`, `signups/`, `checkouts/` + `YYYY-MM-DD/`, see `src/lib/server/events.ts`) in the BLOB_READ_WRITE_TOKEN store, deleted after 35 days by the cron. Download records hold only the page, a `what` label built from menu choices (`src/lib/what.ts`, checked against a vocabulary on the server, never anything typed), page count, member flag, rough place and a random device code (`tl-vid` in localStorage). Since Oct 28, 2026 download and sign-up records also hold where the visit came from (`src` / `via`: a fixed label such as ChatGPT, Google or Email from `src/lib/source.ts`, worked out from the landing page's utm_source and referrer, kept per tab in sessionStorage `tl-src`; never a URL). The digest shows a "Where they came from" table, and the Sunday digest (sent Monday) adds "Last 7 days" by source with the pages AI assistant visitors downloaded from (`week: true` on the admin action adds it to any day). The Monday AI visibility routine reads that email: see `docs/ai-visibility.md`. Manual run: admin action `digest` with `{ date?: "YYYY-MM-DD", send?: true }` (returns the summary; emails only with `send: true`). Tests: `scripts/test-digest.mts`.
- **Env (production):** RESEND_API_KEY (full access), EMAIL_ADMIN_TOKEN, OWNER_EMAIL, RESEND_WEBHOOK_SECRET, CRON_SECRET (digest cron), BLOB_READ_WRITE_TOKEN (already set for the program).

- **Tests:** `scripts/test-email.mts`, `scripts/test-inbound.mts`, `scripts/qa/email_api.py` (mock Resend, see scripts/qa/README.md).
