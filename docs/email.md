# Email (Resend)

How Tracelings email works, for future sessions.

- **Provider:** Resend. Sends from `hello@tracelings.com` (tracelings.com is verified in Resend). Plan: free marketing tier (1,000 contacts, 3 segments; Resend's own "General" segment uses one).
- **Segments:** `prospects` (families, not members, get launch and sales emails) and `teachers` (honest classroom note only). Members are marked with the `member` contact property; the welcome series stops at the next step for members and for anyone who unsubscribes.
- **Sign-ups:** `/api/subscribe/` sends to Zapier (alerts) and Resend (contact + welcome trigger). Never blocks a download.
- **Welcome series:** Resend Automations, one per interest (letters, coloring, math, homeschool, program, general, teacher), days 0, 2, 5, 9, 14. Created switched off; turned on with the admin action `automations-enable` after Jesse approves.
- **Templates:** `src/lib/email/templates.ts`. `setup` pushes and publishes them to Resend. Preview with `npx tsx scripts/email-preview.mts <dir>`.
- **Launch broadcasts** for the imported Oct 2026 list: drafts `launch-a`, `launch-a-teacher`, `launch-b`, `launch-c`. Sent only with `broadcast-send` and `confirm: "SEND"`.
- **Re-creating a launch draft** after the copy changes: `broadcast-create` with the same template refreshes the existing draft of that name in place (no duplicates; pass `fresh: true` to force a new one). A scheduled one is refused: run `broadcast-cancel` with `{ id }` first (Resend `POST /broadcasts/{id}/cancel`: scheduled goes back to draft, queued becomes canceled), then `broadcast-create` again, then `broadcast-send` to reschedule.
- **Copy rule since Oct 24, 2026:** emails never promise the homeschool plans stay free. Use: "Every one-page printable on Tracelings stays free. The full homeschool plans now live inside the membership, and week 1 is always free to try." Devices that used the free plans keep them free through Nov 30, 2026 (launch-a says so).
- **Admin API:** `POST https://tracelings.com/api/admin/email/` with `Authorization: Bearer $EMAIL_ADMIN_TOKEN`. Actions: domain-status, domain-verify, setup, automations-enable/disable, import, broadcast-create, broadcast-cancel, broadcast-send, test-send (only to OWNER_EMAIL), templates, stats, inbound-webhook, welcome-backfill, digest.
- **Replies to hello@tracelings.com:** Resend receiving (enabled on the domain in the Resend dashboard) calls `/api/email/inbound/` (signed with RESEND_WEBHOOK_SECRET), which forwards to OWNER_EMAIL with Reply-To set to the sender.
- **Daily download digest:** one email a day to OWNER_EMAIL from `Tracelings reports <hello@tracelings.com>` with yesterday's (America/Chicago) downloads grouped by visitor, top pages, new sign-ups and checkout starts. Vercel Cron (`vercel.json`, `5 12 * * *` UTC, about 7am Central) calls `GET /api/cron/daily-digest/` with `Authorization: Bearer $CRON_SECRET`; nothing is sent on an empty day unless `?force=1`. Records are small private blobs (`events/`, `signups/`, `checkouts/` + `YYYY-MM-DD/`, see `src/lib/server/events.ts`) in the BLOB_READ_WRITE_TOKEN store, deleted after 35 days by the cron. Download records hold only the page, a `what` label built from menu choices (`src/lib/what.ts`, checked against a vocabulary on the server, never anything typed), page count, member flag, rough place and a random device code (`tl-vid` in localStorage). Since Oct 28, 2026 download and sign-up records also hold where the visit came from (`src` / `via`: a fixed label such as ChatGPT, Google or Email from `src/lib/source.ts`, worked out from the landing page's utm_source and referrer, kept per tab in sessionStorage `tl-src`; never a URL). The digest shows a "Where they came from" table, and the Sunday digest (sent Monday) adds "Last 7 days" by source with the pages AI assistant visitors downloaded from (`week: true` on the admin action adds it to any day). The Monday AI visibility routine reads that email: see `docs/ai-visibility.md`. Since Nov 10, 2026 the sent digest also has an "Email list" section (`src/lib/server/emailHealth.ts`): subscribers, paying members, total unsubscribes, new contacts that day, who unsubscribed since the last digest (worked out from a daily snapshot of unsubscribed contact ids, `email-snap/YYYY-MM-DD.json` in the same Blob store, kept 14 days), the last 3 sent broadcasts from the last 30 days (sent, opens, clicks, unsubscribes; opens or clicks show "off" when tracking is off on the domain) and whether each welcome automation is enabled. It only runs when the digest is actually sent, never on a preview, and never stops the digest (it shows a short note if Resend is unreachable). Tests: `scripts/test-email-health.mts`. Manual run: admin action `digest` with `{ date?: "YYYY-MM-DD", send?: true }` (returns the summary; emails only with `send: true`). Tests: `scripts/test-digest.mts`.
- **Env (production):** RESEND_API_KEY (full access), EMAIL_ADMIN_TOKEN, OWNER_EMAIL, RESEND_WEBHOOK_SECRET, CRON_SECRET (digest cron), BLOB_READ_WRITE_TOKEN (already set for the program).
- **Footer (CAN-SPAM):** Tracelings, 17350 State Hwy 249, Ste 220 #35878, Houston, TX 77064, US, plus Resend's one-click unsubscribe.
- **Tests:** `scripts/test-email.mts`, `scripts/test-inbound.mts`, `scripts/qa/email_api.py` (mock Resend, see scripts/qa/README.md).
- **Christmas storybook broadcasts (Nov 2026):** drafts `xmas-book-a` (intro, send Thanksgiving week) and `xmas-book-b` (reminder, about 9 days later), families (`prospects`) only. Both point to `/personalized-christmas-book/`. Created and sent with the same `broadcast-create` / `broadcast-send` actions, only after Jesse approves.
- **Weekly email (from Nov 22, 2026):** one email every Sunday to families (`prospects`). Content: `src/data/weekly/<Sunday YYYY-MM-DD>.json` (type `WeeklyContent` in `src/lib/email/templates.ts`; checked by `weeklyProblems()`), written each Friday by a scheduled task and pushed to main. Each week: a short intro, 2 to 5 free printables, an optional picture, one paid offer (`feature`: `storybook`, `club` or `program`, rotate them; storybook switches to the Christmas book Nov 1 to Dec 22) and a tip. Vercel Cron `0 23 * * 0` (about 5pm Central) calls `/api/cron/weekly-email/` (CRON_SECRET), which builds the broadcast `Weekly <date>`. Until Jesse has approved 3 weeks, it emails OWNER_EMAIL an `[Approve]` preview with a link to `/api/email/weekly-approve/` (a page with a Send button; the link alone never sends). After 3 approvals it sends automatically. Records in the Blob store: `weekly/approved/<date>.json`, `weekly/sent/<date>.json` (a week is never sent twice). A missing content file emails Jesse "No weekly email for <date>" and sends nothing. Manual re-run: `GET /api/cron/weekly-email/?date=YYYY-MM-DD` with the cron bearer.
- **Storybook Club ($9.99/month, Nov 2026):** separate Square subscription (`CLUB` in `src/lib/server/square.ts`, not in PLANS, so it never counts as a program member). Members download every storybook free (`/api/storybook/club-book/`); program members do too. Sign-up in-page on the storybook pages (`/api/storybook/club-join/`), shown and cancelled on `/account/`.
